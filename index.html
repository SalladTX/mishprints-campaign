How a Slot Works

  1. Back the Main Character tier ($175).
  2. Recommend any Mishprint you want, no checklist required!
  3. Suggest a parody name and two attack names for your card.
  4. Mishprints reviews and approves the final design.
  5. Your name is printed on the card itself!

Final design remains at Mishprints' discretion. Concepts that don't fit the tone or violate copyright/community guidelines may be declined.

FAQ

Is this a donation or a preorder?

It's a preorder. Every tier ships real product: packs, boxes, promo cards. Your money funds artwork and production for Set 2, and you get physical cards in return.

Where do I actually check out?

Checkout happens right on mishprints.com. This page is the campaign hub, every "Preorder" button links straight out to checkout.

How does the card slot / parody name thing work?

15 of the ~80+ cards in Set 2 are left open. Main Character backers ($175) claim one first-come-first-served and recommend any Mishprint they want, no checklist required! Suggest a parody name and two attack names, and get your name printed on the card. Final design remains at Mishprints' discretion. Concepts that don't fit the tone or violate copyright/community guidelines may be declined.

What happens at $7,000?

That's the funding goal! Hitting it drops the retail box price storewide from $65 to $60. Everything on the Stretch Goals ladder below and above that number unlocks in order and stays unlocked for good, no matter what.

Is Mishprints affiliated with Pokémon?

No. Mishprints is an independent, self-published parody card series inspired by the aesthetics of classic TCGs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Pokémon or Nintendo.

Is there a deadline?

Yes! The campaign runs through November 1, 2026. Stretch goals unlock as funding milestones are hit along the way.

How do I get free perks without buying anything?

Follow @mishprintsofficial on Instagram. That's where polls, parody-name submissions, and production updates happen. No purchase required!
