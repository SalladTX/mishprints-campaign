How a Slot Works

  1. Back the Main Character tier ($75) via Big Cartel.
  2. Pick an open Pokémon-parody slot from the current checklist.
  3. Submit your parody name idea for that card.
  4. Mishprints reviews & gives final approval on the name.
  5. Your name is credited to that card in the collector booklet.

FAQ

Is this a donation or a preorder?

It's a preorder. Every tier ships real product — packs, boxes, promo cards. Your money funds artwork and production for Set 2, and you get physical cards in return.

Where do I actually check out?

Checkout happens on our storefront at mishprints.com (Big Cartel). This page is the campaign hub — tiers link straight out to checkout.

How does the card slot / parody name thing work?

15 of the ~80 cards in Set 2 are left open. Main Character backers claim one first-come-first-served and submit a parody name idea. Mishprints has final approval, and your name is credited in the collector booklet either way.

Is Mishprints affiliated with Pokémon?

No. Mishprints is an independent, self-published parody card series inspired by the aesthetics of classic TCGs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Pokémon or Nintendo.

Is there a deadline?

No hard deadline — this is a long-runway campaign. Community Unlocks get added as funding milestones are hit rather than as a ticking-clock stretch goal.

How do I get free perks without buying anything?

Follow @mishprintsofficial on Instagram. That's where polls, parody-name submissions, easter-egg contests, and production updates happen — no purchase required.
